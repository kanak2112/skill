sides of the same session. All of its words are on screen as captions or chapter cards, so it works

## Where to cut in your physical prop

The chapter cards are clean cut points, so live-action shots can go in without re-rendering.
Suggested shots, 2–4 seconds each:

1. **After "Twenty-two years in commercial kitchens":** close-up of Arjun's hands at a chopping
   board, the patch on his forearm and temple. Establishes the real object.
2. **During recording ("He just cooks"):** a busy kitchen pass, the patch catching the light.
3. **After "Five months later":** the patch resting in its dock on a kitchen shelf at night.
4. **After "The one who rents":** the box on a terrace; hands lift the lid; a phone touches it.
5. **After "her hands move like his":** her hands slicing quickly and precisely, the patch at the
   temple, out of focus. This is the shot that sells the fiction.
6. **Before "Asha gets the job":** the patch peeled off and set down next to a chef's knife.
