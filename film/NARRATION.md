## Where to cut in your physical prop

The chapter cards are natural cut points, so live-action shots of the real patch can go in
without re-rendering. Suggested shots, 2–4 seconds each:

1. **After the Day 1 card (0:12):** macro of the patch on a kitchen counter, next to a knife and a
   chopping board. Establishes the object before the website does.
2. **After the Day 2 card (0:58):** the box on a terrace; hands lift the lid; a phone touches it.
3. **During calibration (1:02):** the patch pressed onto a temple, held for ten seconds.
4. **After "For one hour, her hands move like his" (2:24):** hands slicing onions quickly and
   precisely; the patch visible at the temple, out of focus.
5. **After the debrief (2:45):** the patch peeled off and set down in its dock. Then the
   "Asha gets the job" card.

Shot 4 is the one that sells the fiction: the interface tells you what is happening, the real
hands show it.
